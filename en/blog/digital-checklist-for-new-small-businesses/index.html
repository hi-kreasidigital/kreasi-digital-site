A Digital Checklist for Businesses in Their First Two Years

What does a new small business need to be found and bought from online? A step-by-step checklist from Google Business Profile to your own ordering system.

New businesses are usually busy with the products and customers right in front of them. Yet most buyers now search Google, Instagram or WhatsApp before they visit. This checklist is layered so you know what to do now and what can wait.

Layer one: essential, low-cost foundations

  • Google Business Profile. This comes first. Without it, your shop is almost invisible on Google Maps. Add opening hours, photos, category and your WhatsApp number.
  • WhatsApp Business. Turn on the product catalog, auto-replies and chat labels (new, in progress, done). For many small businesses, this is the first online checkout.
  • Instagram Business. Your visual portfolio and social proof, such as testimonials and the feel of your shop.
  • One bio link. A single link in your bio that points to WhatsApp, Google Maps and other ways to order.

Layer two: once the business is steady

  • A simple website. It doesn't need to be a full online store. One page with your profile, best products, location and an order button builds credibility and helps you show up on Google.
  • Basic local SEO. Use the same name, address and phone number everywhere, ask customers for Google reviews, and refresh your photos regularly.
  • TikTok. If your products are visual, short videos can reach new people without a large following.
  • Marketplaces. If your products can be shipped, list them on a marketplace.

Layer three: as the business matures

  • Digital payments such as QRIS.
  • A simple customer database so you can reach past customers with promotions.
  • Reading basic data from Google Business Profile and Instagram, such as peak hours and where visitors come from.

Delivery apps or your own ordering system?

Delivery apps give quick access to many users, with couriers and payments built in. But every transaction carries a commission, and the customer data stays with the app. You can't reach those customers again without going through the app.

Your own ordering system is the opposite. You build traffic gradually, but there's no per-order commission and the customer data is yours. Many businesses use both: apps to reach new customers, their own system for loyal ones.

When is it time to level up?

Start with WhatsApp Business as your online checkout. When orders start slipping through, manual records can't keep up, or app commissions feel heavy, your business is ready for its own ordering system. That's where Kreasi Digital can help design a system that fits how you work.
