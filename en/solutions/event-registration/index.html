Still checking event registrations by hand over chat?

Fun run, city ride or seminar sign-ups over chat with transfers checked one by one? We build registration portals with payment verification and QR check-in.

Discuss on WhatsApp

Why this happens

Community events are often run by volunteers with little time. Sign-ups over chat feel easy at first, but once participants reach the hundreds, the committee drowns in messages, payment receipts and name lists to reconcile.

It peaks on event day: long queues for race packs and volunteers searching spreadsheets for names.

Signs you need a solution

  • Volunteers match payment receipts to names one by one
  • Participants keep asking whether their registration went through
  • Jersey sizes or categories are often wrong
  • Long queues at race pack pickup

Solution options, starting with the simplest

Not every problem needs a big system. We help you choose the step that fits.

  1. 1

    Online registration form

    Participant details, categories and jersey sizes land in one tidy table.

  2. 2

    Payment verification

    Payment status per participant is clear, with automatic confirmation once verified.

  3. 3

    QR code check-in

    Every participant gets a QR code; volunteers just scan it for race pack pickup and attendance.

Yes. The registration system is built to be reused: just change the event name, categories and dates.
