Struggling to reach cafés and restaurants to offer your products?

Supplying pastries, coffee or ingredients but unsure where to start pitching cafés and restaurants? We build a system to map and track outreach to business customers.

Discuss on WhatsApp

Why this happens

Home-based bakeries, roasters and food producers often have great products but struggle to get into cafés and restaurants. The prospect list is scattered, nobody knows who has been contacted, and follow-ups get forgotten.

Selling to businesses is a game of consistency. Without tidy records, warm leads go cold again.

Signs you need a solution

  • Your list of target cafés lives in phone notes or in your head
  • You forget who you've pitched and when
  • Samples were sent, but nobody followed up
  • Each salesperson keeps their own spreadsheet

Solution options, starting with the simplest

Not every problem needs a big system. We help you choose the step that fits.

  1. 1

    Prospect database

    One list of cafés and restaurants by area, with contacts, business type and notes.

  2. 2

    Outreach status tracker

    Each prospect has a status (not contacted, sample sent, negotiating, deal) the whole team can see.

  3. 3

    Online product catalog

    A wholesale catalog page you can send to prospects, with prices and minimum orders.
