WhatsApp orders keep getting missed?

WhatsApp orders buried under other chats, written down wrong or forgotten? We build a simple order system with clear order status for your business.

Discuss on WhatsApp

Why this happens

WhatsApp is the easiest way for customers to order. The problem is that one number handles everything: orders, questions, complaints and personal chats. On busy days, a morning order can sink beneath the afternoon's messages.

The cost is more than one lost order. Customers are let down, staff blame each other, and the owner has to scroll through every chat to know what has and hasn't been done.

Signs you need a solution

  • Customers chase orders that were never started
  • Orders are copied into a notebook before they are processed
  • The team can't tell which orders have been paid
  • The daily order recap takes more than 30 minutes
  • The owner has to keep the phone in hand so nothing slips through

Solution options, starting with the simplest

Not every problem needs a big system. We help you choose the step that fits.

  1. 1

    WhatsApp Business labels and order templates

    The simplest step: order templates, status labels and quick replies so orders are easy to track.

  2. 2

    Online order form

    Customers fill in a short form, orders land in one tidy list, then get confirmed on WhatsApp.

  3. 3

    Order system with status tracking

    Every order has a status (new, in progress, shipped, done) the whole team can see from their phones.

Frequently asked questions

Can customers still order through WhatsApp?

Yes. The goal isn't to replace WhatsApp but to make sure every incoming order is recorded with a status, so nothing slips through.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the scope. After a free conversation, we give you a few options, starting from the simplest, each with a clear cost estimate.

How long does it take?

It depends on complexity. A simple solution is much quicker than a full system. We give you a clear timeline before work begins.

Will you train our team to use it?

Yes. We support your team until they're comfortable using the system day to day.
