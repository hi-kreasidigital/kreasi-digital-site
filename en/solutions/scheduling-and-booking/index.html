Schedules and bookings keep clashing?

Double bookings, staff schedules nobody can see, lost customer history? We build a booking system that shows everyone's free slots in one place.

Chat on WhatsApp

Why this happens

Salons, workshops, clinics, rentals and tour operators have one thing in common: income depends on the schedule. When bookings live in a notebook, a group chat or someone's memory, a clash is only a matter of time.

One double booking means one unhappy customer. One empty slot nobody noticed means money left on the table.

Signs you need a fix

  • Two customers show up at the same time for the same staff member
  • Customers message back and forth just to find a free slot
  • Staff don't know tomorrow's schedule until tonight
  • Customer service history isn't recorded

Your options, starting with the simplest

Not every problem needs a big system. We'll help you pick the step that fits.

  1. 1

    Shared team calendar

    One calendar showing every staff member and room, on any phone.

  2. 2

    Online booking page

    Customers pick a service and an open slot, then get an automatic confirmation.

  3. 3

    Booking system + customer history

    Every booking is saved with service history, reminders and staff commission.

Questions people often ask

Can customers book on their own without messaging first?

Yes. Customers see open slots and pick one themselves, and you get a confirmation. You still control the services and staff hours.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the scope. After a free chat, we'll give you a few options, starting with the simplest, each with a clear cost estimate.

How long does it take?

It depends on how complex it is. A simple fix is much quicker than a full system. We'll give you a clear timeline before we start.

Will you show our team how to use it?

Yes. We stick with your team until they're comfortable using it day to day.
