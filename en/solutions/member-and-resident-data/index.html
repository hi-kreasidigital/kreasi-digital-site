Member or resident data scattered across files?

Member, dues or resident data still spread across spreadsheets? We build a central database that's easy to search, summarize and hand over.

Discuss on WhatsApp

Why this happens

Associations, communities and village administrators all manage data about people: who the members are, who is active, who qualifies for assistance. That data usually grows across many files made by different committee members.

When it's needed, such as the number of voters aged 17+ or the list of aid recipients, someone has to merge and recount it by hand.

Signs you need a solution

  • Several versions of the member list exist and nobody knows which is current
  • Counting people who meet certain criteria takes hours
  • Archives go missing when the committee changes
  • Issuing member cards or certificates is slow

Solution options, starting with the simplest

Not every problem needs a big system. We help you choose the step that fits.

  1. 1

    Central database

    One member or resident table with agreed fields, accessible to the committee from any phone.

  2. 2

    Summary dashboard

    Counts by category (age, status, area, aid recipients) calculated automatically.

  3. 3

    Member portal

    Dues status, digital member cards and certificates members can download themselves.
