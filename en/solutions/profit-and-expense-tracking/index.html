Not sure how much your business really earns and spends?

Busy sales but the money doesn't show? We build automatic sales and expense tracking so your real profit is clear.

Discuss on WhatsApp

Why this happens

Many owners feel sales are strong, yet at month's end the money isn't there. The cause is almost always the same: business and personal money are mixed, small expenses go unrecorded, and sales are remembered rather than recorded.

Without reliable numbers, big decisions like raising prices, hiring or opening a branch are made on gut feeling.

Signs you need a solution

  • You can't state last month's profit with confidence
  • Small expenses (fuel, packaging, phone credit) are never recorded
  • Business and personal money share one wallet or account
  • Financial reports only get made when you need a loan

Solution options, starting with the simplest

Not every problem needs a big system. We help you choose the step that fits.

  1. 1

    Daily cash log

  2. 2

    Digital POS with automatic reports

  3. 3

Frequently asked questions

I don't know accounting. Can I still use it?

Yes. Recording is kept as simple as possible: just log money in and out, and the profit and loss report is calculated for you.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the scope. After a free conversation, we give you a few options, starting from the simplest, each with a clear cost estimate.

How long does it take?

It depends on complexity. A simple solution is much quicker than a full system. We give you a clear timeline before work begins.

Will you train our team to use it?

Yes. We support your team until they're comfortable using the system day to day.
